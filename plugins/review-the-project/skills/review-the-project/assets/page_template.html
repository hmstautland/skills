    Open decisions

    Method

    Each finding was checked by a second, independent reviewer agent, told to disprove it by tracing the code. Verified means it held up. Verified, corrected means it held up, but the second reviewer corrected details such as the mechanism, the location or the severity; the corrected statement is shown on the finding. Findings that did not hold up were dropped and are not on this page.
